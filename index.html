Delete your Circalune account

Circalune is the family location app. This page explains how to ask for your account and the data stored with it to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Circalune and check the email address you use to log in.
  2. From that same email address, send a message with the subject Delete my Circalune account.
  3. Send it to alex.brazer1@gmail.com.
  4. Circalune confirms the request came from the account email, then deletes the account. You will get a short reply when it is done.

We cannot delete an account from a different email address, because that is how we check the request is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, and account.
  • Your circle, saved places, and the location history stored with your account.
  • Your trip log, opening kilometres, and vehicle or taxpayer details saved in the app.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of a confirmed request.
  • Circalune does not keep a copy of the account after that.
  • Google may keep the Play Store record of the R50 membership, because Google processes that payment, not Circalune.
  • People who already saw your live location in your circle will still have what was shown on their own phones. New location updates stop as soon as the account is deleted.
